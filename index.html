FAQ

Questions we're often asked

How much roof space do I need?

As a rule of thumb, about 80–100 sq ft of shade-free area per kW. A typical 3–5 kW home system needs roughly 300–500 sq ft. Our survey measures your actual usable area.

What's the difference between on-grid, off-grid and hybrid?

On-grid systems feed extra power to the electricity grid through a net meter and give the fastest payback, but switch off during power cuts. Off-grid systems run on batteries without the grid. Hybrid systems do both — net metering plus battery backup for outages.

Is there a government subsidy for rooftop solar?

Residential rooftop systems may qualify for central and state subsidy schemes, such as PM Surya Ghar. Eligibility and amounts change, so we check what applies to you and handle the application.

How long does installation take?

Most home systems are installed within a few days once material is on site. Net meter approval depends on your electricity board; we follow it up until it's done.

What maintenance does a solar system need?

Mainly regular panel cleaning — every few weeks in dusty areas — and a yearly check of wiring, earthing and the inverter. We offer cleaning visits and annual maintenance contracts.

What warranties do I get?

Panels usually carry a long manufacturer performance warranty (often 25 years), and inverters typically 5–10 years depending on the brand. We share the exact warranty terms for every component in your proposal.
