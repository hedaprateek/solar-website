What we do

Every part of your solar project, under one roof

No juggling separate vendors for the survey, the structure, the panels and the wiring. One team owns the job from start to finish — and stays on for service afterwards.

01

Site & area survey

We visit, measure usable roof or ground area, check shading through the day and study your electricity bills to size the system correctly.

  • Shadow & orientation analysis
  • Load and bill study
  • Roof and structure assessment
02

Structure fabrication

Mounting structures fabricated for your site — RCC roof, tin shed or open ground — set at the right tilt and built to hold through wind and monsoon.

  • Galvanised steel & aluminium
  • Standard & elevated structures
  • Ground-mount and carports
03

Panel selection & supply

We recommend the right panel technology and wattage for your space and budget, and supply genuine modules with manufacturer warranty.

  • Mono PERC, TOPCon & bifacial
  • Reputed, warrantied brands
  • Sized to your actual load
04

Installation & fitting

Trained crews mount, align and fix every panel safely, route cables neatly and leave you with a clean, finished site.

  • Safe rooftop working practices
  • Correct tilt and alignment
  • Tidy cable management
05

Inverters & batteries

On-grid, off-grid and hybrid inverters with optional battery backup, matched to your panels and to how you use power.

  • On-grid, off-grid & hybrid
  • Lithium and lead-acid backup
  • Mobile app monitoring
06

Electrical installation

Complete AC and DC side work done to standard, with proper protection, earthing and lightning safety included — not left as extras.

  • ACDB / DCDB protection boxes
  • Earthing & lightning arrestor
  • Wiring to your main board
07

Net metering & approvals

We handle the paperwork with your electricity board for net metering and help you apply for any subsidy you are eligible for.

  • DISCOM application & follow-up
  • Net meter installation
  • Subsidy application support
08

Maintenance & AMC

Scheduled cleaning and health checks keep your system producing what it should, year after year.

  • Panel cleaning
  • Periodic inspection
  • Annual maintenance contracts
09

Repairs & upgrades

System not performing? We find and fix faults, replace inverters and expand existing installations — even ones we didn't install.

  • Fault diagnosis
  • Inverter replacement
  • Capacity expansion

How we work

From survey to switch-on in six clear steps

You always know what happens next, who is doing it and when.

  1. Site survey

    We visit your site, measure the area, check shading and structure, and go through your electricity bills.

  2. Design & proposal

    A system designed for your load and space, with the component list, price and timeline in writing.

  3. Fabrication

    Mounting structures are fabricated to your site's measurements and the right tilt angle.

  4. Installation

    Panels, inverter, protection boxes, earthing and wiring are installed by our own crew.

  5. Commissioning & net meter

    We test and switch the system on, then follow up with the electricity board until the net meter is in place.

  6. After-sales care

    Cleaning, health checks and quick support whenever you need it — one call away.
